Statement of Faith

The following statements summarize the core beliefs of God's Awe Worship Ministries.

01

The Holy Scriptures

We believe that the bible is the inspired, authoritative, inerrant, and infallible word of God. (2 Peter 1:21; 2 Timothy 3:16)

02

The Holy Trinity

Believe in the holy trinity, the God head consisting of the father, the son and the Holy spirit. These three are one. (2 Corinthians 13:14)

03

Jesus Christ

3. We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ (that he is God). His virgin birth, his atoning death, his bodily resurrection 2nd his ascension into heaven (John10:30; 17:5 Philippians 2:6; colossians1:19; Titus 2:13 1Tmothy 3:16) We believe in the personal and the pre-millennial return of Jesus Christ to receive unto himself red as a “Rupture”. We believe that Christ will reign on earth for one thousand years(millennial) with his saints. (1 thessalonias4:16-17, Titus2:13, Revelation20:4; Revalation21and22)

04

The Church

We believe that the universal church and the local church are the body of Jesus Christ, the habitation of God through the spirit with divine appointments for the fulfilment of the great commission. Each believer born of the spirit is an integral part of being the general assembly and church of the first born of the spirit which are written in heaven and that individual members of the local church must come together for worship and fellowship. (Ephesians1:22-23, Hebrews10:25).

05

Salvation & Holy Spirit

We believe in the personal salvation through Jesus Christ, upon the confession and belief in one’s heart and that it is the work of God’s grace. We also believe in the baptism and infilling with the Holy Ghost with the initial evidence of speaking in other tongues and the manifesting the fruit of spirit thereof. (Isaiah 28:11, Luke24:29, Acts2:1-4, Romans10:9-10, Ephesians2:8, Acts10:46, Galatians56:22-16).

06

Water Baptism

We believe in the believers’ baptism in water by immersion as public testimony to the world of the inward work of the grace of God. Immersion and the subsequent raising from the water are symbolic of the Christian dying to sin and to the world and raising with Jesus unto newness of life. Baptism is in the name father, the son and the holy spirit. We do not consider baptism which takes place prior to the individuals personal experience of salvation as scriptural. (matthew28:19, Romans6:4)

07

Divine Healing

.We believe in the divine healing and that it is obtained based on the atonement and faith in the healer Jesus Christ. (Matthew 9:12, mark2:17, mark6:13, james5:14-15,1peter2:24, exodus2:26, isaiah53). We also believe in medical care.

08

Satan & Final Judgment

We believe in the existence of Satan and the Demons. We believe that the devil, Satan, does not possess the attributes of God and God restrains him. We believe in the casting out of Demons, also we believe that they together with those whose names are not written in the book of life will finally be judged by God and consigned to the everlasting punishment in the lake of fire which burns with fire and brimstone. (Revelation 19:20, 20:10-15

About Us

This ministry is called to share the Gospel of Jesus, disciple nation, raise a generation who shall worship the father in spirit and truth.

Gods Awe Worship Ministries was begun as a prayer fellowship in January 2020 by Rev. Jeremy and Tabitha Mwenda. By God's grace on September 27, 2020, it was established as a ministry based In Abilene Texas, United states of America. Since its inception, the ministry has continued to attract and minister to large numbers of people, locally and globally,both young and old in all walks of life. The name God's Awe Worship Center Ministries was prayerfully derived from Hebrews 12:28 "Therefore, let us be grateful for receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, and thus let us offer bo God acceptable worship, with reverence and Awe for our God is a consuming fire." The name reflects the main ministry and vision of the church. From that humble beginning, the ministry has grown and developed in diverse ways to the glory of God the father, his son Jesus Christ and the Holy Spirit, and the extension of his kingdom Amen.
